Grammar Cheat Sheet

Short Adjectives (-er than)

short ➔ shorter than | fast ➔ faster than | hard ➔ harder than

Long Adjectives (more... than)

boring ➔ more boring than | interesting ➔ more interesting than

Like vs. Would Like

Like: General preferences ➔ I like reading books.

Would like / I'd like: Specific requests ➔ I'd like two tickets, please.
